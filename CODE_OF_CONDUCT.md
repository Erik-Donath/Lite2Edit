# Code of Conduct

Lite2Edit welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person’s private information.
- Follow the project’s license and attribute work appropriately.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close contributions that violate these expectations and may block repeat offenders. To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer at [github@erikdonath.de](mailto:github@erikdonath.de).
